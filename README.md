# Company Intelligence Research Agent

An AI-powered company research agent built with Google ADK and SerpApi.

The agent searches the web for current company information and generates structured intelligence reports instead of relying only on the AI model's stored knowledge.

## Features

- 🔎 Current web research using SerpApi
- 🏢 Company overview research
- 📰 Latest company news
- 🤖 AI and technology research
- 💼 Job and hiring intelligence
- 🏆 Competitor research
- ⚖️ Company comparison
- 🔗 Source links for researched information
- 🧠 Automatic research planning based on the user's goal

## How It Works

User
↓
Google ADK Agent
↓
Research Planner
↓
SerpApi Tools
↓
Google Search / Google Jobs
↓
Current Web Information
↓
AI-generated Company Intelligence Report
